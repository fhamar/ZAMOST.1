Delivery, Return & Order Rules

🚚 Delivery Policy

  • Normal estimated delivery: 3–7 working days after dispatch/order processing.
  • Remote or selected locations may take approximately 5–10 working days.
  • Delivery time is an estimate, not a guaranteed exact date.
  • Delivery may be delayed because of weather, transport disruption, holidays, operational issues or remote locations.
  • The customer must provide a correct name, mobile number and complete delivery address.
Order status can be checked from the Track Order page when tracking information is available.

↩️ Return & Refund Policy

  • Return requests are considered for eligible wrong, damaged or defective products.
  • The product should be kept in its original condition, with available packaging, tags and accessories.
  • Return eligibility may depend on the product category and the condition of the item.
  • After verification, an eligible item may be replaced or refunded according to the applicable order/payment process.
  • Normal change-of-mind returns may not be available for every product.
Do not send a product back without first submitting a support/return request and receiving instructions.

❌ Order Cancellation

  • Cancellation can be requested before the order is dispatched.
  • Once an order has entered dispatch/shipping, cancellation may no longer be possible.
  • For prepaid orders, any eligible refund is processed according to the payment/refund process.
  • Cancellation requests should include the Order ID.

📦 Replacement Policy

  • Wrong, damaged or defective items should be reported as soon as possible after delivery.
  • Support may ask for order information and reasonable product evidence for verification.
  • Replacement depends on product availability and verification.
  • If replacement is unavailable, an eligible refund may be considered.

🚨 Damaged / Wrong Product

  • Check the parcel and product when received.
  • If the item is damaged, wrong or defective, report it through Report a Problem.
  • Keep the product, packaging and included accessories until the support case is resolved.
  • Do not modify, misuse or intentionally damage an item after delivery.

👤 Customer Responsibility

  • Provide accurate delivery and contact information.
  • Be available or arrange an authorized receiver when delivery is attempted.
  • Keep Order ID and payment information available for support queries.
  • Do not share your account password or authentication codes with anyone.
